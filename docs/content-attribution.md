# Content Attribution and Non-Commercial Scope

## Current product decision

Terraria Daily Guess is currently a non-commercial quality-focused prototype.
Advertising, affiliate links, sponsorships, paid features, paid traffic, and
analytics that monetize users are out of scope. They must not be added without
a new product, privacy, security, and licensing review.

## Official Terraria Wiki research

The Official Terraria Wiki at `terraria.wiki.gg` states that page content is
available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless otherwise noted. That license permits
sharing and adaptation for non-commercial use when attribution, a license link,
change indication, and ShareAlike obligations are respected.

For this project, wiki material may be used only as manually reviewed research
for original clue writing. The importer must never scrape wiki pages, cache an
export, copy tables, reproduce prose, or download wiki media. Game images,
sprites, logos, and other materials marked as Re-Logic copyright are excluded.

Every curated clue informed by the wiki must record the exact page URL, page
title, access date, the string `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`, and the reviewer in
`data/enrichment/curated-clues.json`. A public release using such adapted
content needs a visible attribution page that links to the source material and
the license and identifies the project text as adapted/originally rewritten.

## Open licensing boundary

This policy does not apply a Creative Commons license to the project's source
code. Before publishing an enrichment dataset derived from wiki material,
review whether it is an adaptation or a collection and decide the appropriate
ShareAlike treatment for that dataset. This is a licensing review requirement,
not legal advice.
